2. HOW TO USE — FOR STUDENTS & TEST TAKERS
================================================================================

STEP 1: Open the Link
- Tap or click the link above, or scan the QR code from the welcome screen.

STEP 2: Enter Your Full Name
- Type your full name in the "Full Name" input box.
- The bright orange "Start Quiz 🚀" button will unlock. Click it to begin!

STEP 3: Taking the Quiz (50 Questions / 45 Minutes)
- Timer: 45 minutes recommended (counted down in the top-right corner).
  * Note: If 45 minutes expires, you will NOT be locked out. An overtime
    banner appears, allowing you to finish all remaining questions peacefully.

- Quiz Structure (5 Distinct Parts):
  * PART 1 (Questions 1–10): Multiple Choices
    Select the best subordinating conjunction (A, B, C, or D).
  * PART 2 (Questions 11–20): Paragraph Cloze
    Read the contextual passage (Civil Servants / Military Personnel)
    and pick the conjunction that logically connects the clauses.
  * PART 3 (Questions 21–30): Grammatical Correctness
    Analyze the complex sentence and tap "CORRECT" or "INCORRECT".
  * PART 4 (Questions 31–40): Word Bank Questions
    Complete 2 paragraphs (10 blanks) using words from the 12-word Word Bank.
    Enforces a strict "One-Word-Only" rule (selected words disable in other blanks).
  * PART 5 (Questions 41–50): Sentence Word Ordering / Jumbled Words
    Tap the colorful word chips in order to construct the grammatically
    correct complex sentence. Tap chips in the target box to return them.

STEP 4: Question Palette & Navigation
- Tap "📋 Review Questions" at the top to open the slide-out Question Palette.
- Quickly jump to any question (1 to 50).
- Flag questions (🚩) to revisit before final submission.

STEP 5: Submitting & Reviewing Results
- On Question 50 (or from the confirmation modal), click "Submit Exam".
- View your final score (out of 100 scaled points / 50 raw points), accuracy percentage, and time status.
- Review detailed pedagogical explanations for all 50 questions to learn
  the grammatical rules and correct usage.
